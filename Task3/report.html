7. Risk Prioritization

Vulnerabilities should be prioritized using more than CVSS alone. Important factors include:

  • Whether the affected service is exposed
  • Exploitability
  • Whether exploitation is actively occurring
  • Importance of the affected asset
  • Availability of a patch or mitigation
  • Potential impact

8. Remediation Plan

  1. Update the operating system and installed packages.
  2. Remove or disable unnecessary services.
  3. Restrict exposed services with firewall rules where appropriate.
  4. Apply vendor-recommended security patches.
  5. Review insecure configurations identified by the scanner.
  6. Re-run the vulnerability scan after remediation.
  7. Compare the new results with the original scan.
