## Phishing Email Sample


From: Microsoft Account Security <security-alert@email.com>
To: amannn@hotmail.com
Date: August 29, 2026
Subject: URGENT: Unusual Sign-In Attempt – Action Required

Dear User,

We detected an unusual sign-in attempt on your Microsoft account
from an unknown device.

For your security, your account may be temporarily restricted.

Please verify your account within 24 hours to prevent interruption
of your Microsoft services.

[http://microsof.security-alert/amannn/login/crenditials]

If you do not verify your account within the required time,
access to your email and other services may be suspended.

Thank you,
Microsoft Account Security Team
